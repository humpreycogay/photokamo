About

A darkroom in your browser.

PhotoKamo is a privacy-first photo playground. Open one photo or many, give them a look, adjust, crop and frame them, or arrange them as grids, shaped stickers, contact sheets and scrapbook collages. Export one photo, a batch or a finished collage. Images and edits stay on your device; there is no upload, account, analytics, or backend. Open photos, their edits and collages are kept in this browser so a reload doesn’t lose them, until you clear them below or close every photo.

The Basic looks are creative approximations, not exact reproductions. Film looks use Kamo, PhotoKamo’s calibrated spectral engine, and run entirely in your browser. Kamo’s calibration assets download only when you choose a film look.

The Lens tab’s camera effects (distortion, colour fringing, light falloff, diffusion, bloom, lens flare, simulated depth of field and bokeh, diffraction) are PhotoKamo’s own code, written from the textbook optics below. Lens profiles are illustrative, not measurements of real lenses, and depth of field comes from the focus area you draw, because a photo holds no depth.
