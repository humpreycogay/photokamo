About

A darkroom in your browser.

PhotoKamo is a privacy-first photo playground. Images and edits stay on your device; there is no upload, account, analytics, or backend.

The Basic looks are creative approximations, not exact reproductions. The film engines run real film simulations entirely in your browser, downloaded only when you choose one: Spektrafilm (spektrafilm by Andrea Volpato, via the Rust port spektrafilm-rs), Negs (the spektrafilm model on WebGPU) and DuXun (a DaVinci Resolve film plugin, ported to JavaScript).

PhotoKamo is free software under the GNU General Public License v3. Each film engine keeps its own licence and credits (choose it under Engine in Looks and use ⓘ next to it).
