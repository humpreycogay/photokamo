## 2. Standards tables — third-party, NOT covered by CC BY

Upstream's licence explicitly excludes `data/standards/`: these are tables
"reproduced in order to compute with", which retain the rights of their
publishers. Here that means:

- `StatusM_ISO5-3.json`, `StatusA_ISO5-3.json` — ISO 5-3 Status M / Status A
  spectral products (ISO; the Status M values were compiled upstream via
  ArgyllCMS);
- `CIE1931_2deg_CMFs.json`, `D50_illuminant.json` — CIE 1931 2° observer and
  CIE illuminant D50 (CIE).

**Commercial distribution of these four files needs its own licence review**
(or replacement with copies obtained under known terms). They are kept here
unmodified with hashes so they can be swapped without code changes.

## How Kamo adapts the data

Kamo's spectral engine (`src/spectral/`) reads these files at runtime and:

- resamples every series to a 400–740 nm, 5 nm grid (responsivities in log
  space); dye and D-min spectra that end at 700 nm are held flat to 740 nm;
- converts log spectral sensitivity to linear and balances layers under the
  stock's design illuminant;
- anchors normal exposure at the VISION3 sheets' camera-stops zero, or at an
  ISO-style speed point offset (C-41) or a Status A grey density (E-6);
- converts each sheet's neutral series once into per-layer dye-amount tables
  (Status M/A Gauss-Newton inversion), for film and print materials alike;
- fits each MTF curve as a constrained bank of Gaussian blurs;
- substitutes a related stock's spectral sensitivity where none is published
  (Pro 400H, Ektachrome E100, Provia 100F, Velvia 50/100), flagged in
  `src/spectral/catalog.js`.
